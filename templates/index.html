The problem

Miscommunication isn't a vocabulary gap — it's a barrier that keeps people from understanding each other at all. Most translation tools handle typed text in a handful of major languages. Spoken dialects, sign gesture, and non-phonetic scripts are often left out entirely.

How it works

Three steps, one translation

  1. 1

    Input

    The user provides audio, text, or an image — whatever form the message is already in.

  2. 2

    AI processing

    Lingo identifies the source language, then draws on its understanding of that language to interpret meaning rather than translate word-for-word.

  3. 3

    Output

    Lingo returns English text that carries the intent of the original message — a translation or a transcript, depending on the input.
